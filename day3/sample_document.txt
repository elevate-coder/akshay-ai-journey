Generative AI is transforming enterprise technology.
It helps businesses automate knowledge work, generate content,
summarize documents, classify information, and improve decision-making.

RAG, or Retrieval Augmented Generation, allows an AI system to search
relevant documents before generating an answer. This improves accuracy
and reduces hallucination.

Enterprise GenAI solutions must consider security, privacy, governance,
cost, observability, and responsible AI principles.
