Akshay is the Founder and Principal AI Advisor at TravAI Digital, an independent AI governance,
assurance, and applied AI advisory practice based in Singapore.

He previously served as Director of Gen AI Services for APAC and MEA AI Governance Lead at NTT DATA.
In this role he led delivery of large-scale agentic workforce transformation programs and owned
multi-country AI governance frameworks aligned to MAS, IMDA, PDPA, and emerging regional regulation.

Prior to that, he was Chief Customer Officer at Anchanto, where he grew the business to SGD 16M ARR
across 225 enterprise customers in 10 countries. Before that, he led a 45-person Managed Security
Services Engineering organization at NTT Communications, delivering SGD 20M+ in statements of work.

Akshay holds the AIGP certification from IAPP, passed in April 2026, along with CISM from ISACA
(2020) and SAFe 4 Agilist. He completed the NUS Accelerated Management Programme and NTU
FlexiMasters in Business AI and Technology in 2025. He has approximately 20 years of experience
in technology consulting and program delivery across APAC and MEA.
