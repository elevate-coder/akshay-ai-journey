# AI Governance Frameworks — Working Notes

## NIST AI Risk Management Framework
Voluntary framework structured around four functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage.
Widely referenced by US enterprises and increasingly by APAC organizations benchmarking
against a recognized standard even outside US jurisdiction.

## ISO/IEC 42001
The first international management system standard for AI. Certifiable, similar in structure
to ISO 27001. Organizations pursuing AI governance maturity increasingly ask vendors and
partners whether they hold or are pursuing this certification.

## Singapore-specific
MAS FEAT principles (Fairness, Ethics, Accountability, Transparency) govern AI use in financial
services. IMDA's Model AI Governance Framework provides broader guidance for AI deployment
across sectors, with the AI Verify testing framework as a practical assurance tool.
